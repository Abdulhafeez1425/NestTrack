# NestTrack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-13  
**Owner:** AbdulHafeez Olabisi  
**Contact:** support@nesttrack.app

This Privacy Policy explains how NestTrack collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use the NestTrack platform and related services.

## 1. Information we collect

We may collect information needed to operate the service, including:

- account information such as name, email address, phone number, role, and profile details;
- organization, property, tenancy, and unit information;
- rent and payment evidence, such as payment references, receipts, and submission records;
- maintenance information, including tickets, assignments, comments, and work history;
- messages, notifications, support conversations, and related activity records; and
- technical information such as device, browser, IP address, and log data required for security and support.

We do not store card details or full bank-account numbers in the app. If you upload documents, keep only the information necessary for the task.

## 2. How we use your information

We use information to:

- create and manage accounts and access permissions;
- provide property, tenancy, rent, and maintenance workflows;
- support communication between users and teams;
- review payment evidence and maintenance records;
- improve security, reliability, and customer support; and
- comply with legal obligations and protect the platform.

## 3. Information sharing

We may share information with:

- authorized members of your organization;
- trusted service providers that help us operate the platform;
- law enforcement or regulators when required by law; and
- others when required to protect the rights, safety, or security of users.

We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. We use reasonable security measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also ask to limit or object to certain processing activities.

## 6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the owner at support@nesttrack.app.
